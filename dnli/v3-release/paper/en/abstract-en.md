## Abstract

We study how much a language model’s stated probability changes after one sentence of direction-controlled evidence. Everyday defeasible inference in δ-SNLI supplies a premise–hypothesis pair, a strengthener and a weakener, with annotated direction but no numerical ground-truth probability. Priors and posteriors are elicited in independent calls, crossing stated versus anonymous reported wording with two methods from the Multi-Candidate Verbalization (MCV) diagnostic framework: Direct single-estimate elicitation (D0) versus Multi-Candidate Weighted elicitation (MCW; five weighted estimates, V0, aggregated via the normalized weighted mean M_W). Directional movement s averages changes signed by the annotated direction, so the prior cancels out. Hypotheses and analysis rules were frozen before formal runs, without public registration.

The formal design comprises three primary configurations (Qwen3-8B thinking off and OLMo-2-7B locally, 240 pairs each; gpt-6.1-sol through codex, 120 pairs; 6,600 planned units), four CLI-harnessed dnli-agy configurations (gpt-oss-120b, claude-sonnet-5.5, gemini-3.8-flash low/high; 5,600 units), and eight un-harnessed dnli-api configurations (11,200 units, 11,188 valid; 99.89%). The latter cover Google Gemini Flash-Lite and Gemma dense/MoE models plus SiliconFlow Qwen, GLM and Xing models on the shared 120-pair formal subset (Block-A) each. An exploratory 20-configuration ladder spans eight providers and 3B–550B total parameters (6,000 planned dev units). A controlled 1+1+4 diagnostic uses gpt-4o-mini and gpt-4.1-mini (1,800 dev calls) to compare Direct (D0), single-estimate chain-of-thought (Direct-CoT, D_CoT), MCW (V0), unconstrained-weight MCW (V1), Multi-Candidate Unweighted elicitation (MCU; prompt-level V2 without weights and readout-level unweighted mean M_U), and bare-JSON MCW (V3).

Across all 15 formal configurations (23,400 planned units), H1 rejects with movements of +16.1 to +28.9 points, and H4a rejects with endpoint-rate reductions of +0.065 to +0.391 proportion (6.5–39.1 percentage points). H2 rejects in 13 of 15 (+2.0 to +8.9 points, accompanied by higher reversal rates under reported wording), but is not established for gpt-oss-120b (+0.9) or sf:qwen3.5-4b (−0.64). MCW directional costs (H4b) vary from −13.7 to +0.5 points. Across 14 of the 15 formal configurations (all except OLMo-2-7B), exploratory candidate readings strictly obey Top-1 > MCW (M_W) > MCU (M_U), consistent with verbalized weights protecting directional sensitivity against tail candidates relative to unweighted averaging. Item-level decomposition shows that 44.9%–77.3% of single-update reversals under Stated Direct are low-amplitude (|Δ| ≤ 10 points) and 53.8%–84.2% preserve pair ordering (s_i ≥ 0), while open-weight models paired with MCW retain 19.4%–36.7% mode-preserving tail counterfactuals (TailCF%, reaching 28.6%–36.7% in sf:glm-4-9b, Qwen3-8B, sf:qwen3-8b, aistudio:gemma-4-26b-a4b-it, and gpt-oss-120b) compared with 5.8%–15.8% in the four narrow-spread CLI-harnessed frontier configurations (claude-sonnet-5.5 5.8%, codex 13.3%, gemini-3.8-flash high/low 13.3%/15.8%). On common Block-A items, API Qwen3 closely tracks local Qwen3 (H1 +18.72 vs. +18.90, H4a +0.265 vs. +0.254, H2 +3.78 vs. +1.67, H4b −9.03 vs. −6.70); H1, H4a and H4b reject in both. Local H2 rejects on the full 240-pair split (+1.97), but not on the 120-pair subset. Formal API Gemma 31B (H4b −2.85; Top-1 +0.64; spread −13.3% / +7.6%) and dev Groq gpt-oss-120b (H4b −4.15; Top-1 +0.40; spread −18.4% / +12.2%) demonstrate that MCW costs below the 5-point SESOI and stated narrowing/reported widening occur without CLI harnesses.

In the OpenAI ablation, Direct-CoT (D_CoT) improves polarity discrimination (ROC-AUC 0.9189 / 0.9201) but leaves non-copy first-numeric-token Shannon entropy below 1 bit (0.903 / 0.683 bits, versus 0.765 / 0.649 in D0), with small proposition binary entropy gains (+0.064 / +0.060 bits; endpoint mass 0.087 / 0.107 vs. 0.133 / 0.180 in D0) and mean signed probability differences vs. D0 of −2.37 / −1.83 points. Both MCW (V0) and prompt-level MCU (V2) raise non-copy numeric-token entropy, while draft-then-copy responses (76.0% of gpt-4.1-mini V0 calls versus 26.0% in V2) complicate raw token-entropy interpretation. Prompt-level MCU (V2) shows smaller movement loss on these two smaller models, but compresses within-call candidate spread by more than half (6.59 / 7.85 versus 15.23 / 22.26 points in MCW V0) and halves proposition binary entropy gain (+0.097 / +0.101 versus +0.211 / +0.239 bits). These exploratory comparisons do not identify a causal model-scale effect. Reduced endpoint values and preserved evidence sensitivity must be assessed separately. Because δ-NLI annotates only direction, we make no claim about appropriate update magnitude.
