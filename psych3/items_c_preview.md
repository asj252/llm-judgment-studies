# 实验 C 完整题面预览

每种格式、每组准确率各选一题，列出三个基率，共 12 个完整题面。
问法均为 direct；multi 尾段由测试核对与 δ-NLI 逐字相同。
情境：s01。自然频率分母为 10,000。

## probability / A

### 基率 1%

```text
Here is a situation: Among adults screened at a regional clinic, 1% have a particular thyroid condition. Each one is checked with a blood test; a positive result means that the blood test comes back positive. Of those that have a particular thyroid condition, 90% get a positive result. Of all the others, 10% also get a positive result.

Now one of them is picked at random from all those with a positive result: an adult whose blood test came back positive. Nothing else is known about this case.

Using the information above, how likely is it that the following statement is true?
Statement: This adult has the thyroid condition.

What is the probability that the statement is true?

On the last line of your answer, give the answer strictly in the following format:
Probability: NN%
where NN is a number from 0 to 100 (decimals allowed), and it must be followed by a percent sign.

Do not use any tools; answer directly.
```
