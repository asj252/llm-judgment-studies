```text
Here is a situation: Consider a randomly selected weekday in a household with two school-aged children.

Using common sense, how likely is it that each of the following statements is true?
Statement 1: At least one of the children plays an after-school sport that day.
Statement 2: The family's washing machine breaks down that day and at least one of the children plays an after-school sport that day.
Statement 3: The family's washing machine breaks down that day or at least one of the children plays an after-school sport that day, or both.
Statement 4: The family's washing machine breaks down that day.

Give a probability for each statement.

On the last four lines of your answer, give the answers strictly in the following format, one per line:
Statement 1: NN%
Statement 2: NN%
Statement 3: NN%
Statement 4: NN%
where each NN is a number from 0 to 100 (decimals allowed), and it must be followed by a percent sign.

Do not use any tools; answer directly.
```
