## E01 (mobile plan — Sam, target=P)

### 中性 / 直接 (neutral, direct)

```text
Sam needs to choose between two options. Sam is choosing a mobile phone plan.

Option P: $25 per month; 8 GB of data; no international calling
Option Q: $38 per month; 40 GB of data; free calls to 50 countries

Both options are available. Switching between them costs nothing and can be undone at any time.

What is the probability that Option P is the better choice for Sam?

On the last line of your answer, give the answer strictly in the following format:
Probability: NN%
where NN is a number from 0 to 100 (decimals allowed), and it must be followed by a percent sign.

Do not use any tools; answer directly.
```

### 现状目标 / 直接 (status_T, direct)

```text
Sam needs to choose between two options. Sam is choosing a mobile phone plan.

Option P: $25 per month; 8 GB of data; no international calling
Option Q: $38 per month; 40 GB of data; free calls to 50 countries

Sam is currently on Option P. Sam was assigned to it at random when signing up; the assignment did not take Sam's situation or preferences into account.

Both options are available. Switching between them costs nothing and can be undone at any time.

What is the probability that Option P is the better choice for Sam?

On the last line of your answer, give the answer strictly in the following format:
Probability: NN%
where NN is a number from 0 to 100 (decimals allowed), and it must be followed by a percent sign.

Do not use any tools; answer directly.
```
